Series Overview

Visions of the end of the world can fill us with anxiety and fear. Yet for Christians, the end times are not something to dread. In the book of Revelation, God has given a preview of what is to come so that we might confidently trust Him with our future.

In this ten-session series, Dr. Tony Evans walks through the book of Revelation verse by verse, uncovering God’s final plan for human history and our eternal destiny. This is more than a strange book of symbols—it is a call to worship God and live faithfully in the present.

The purpose of Revelation is to tell Christians how we ought to live in light of things to come, and to tell non-Christians why they need to take Christ seriously in light of things to come.

“What is Satan’s ultimate goal? His goal is to be like the Most High. He wants the world to worship him.
A question that we face today… is who are you worshiping?”

— from the official trailer

Ten Sessions

This companion organizes the book into ten logical sessions that mirror a verse-by-verse journey. Full video sessions and the official free study guide are available only on RightNow Media. Use these outlines, questions, and applications alongside the videos or for personal reflection.
